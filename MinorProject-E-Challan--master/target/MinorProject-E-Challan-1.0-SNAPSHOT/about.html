About FineSnap

FineSnap is a smart digital e-Challan management system developed for police officers to efficiently record traffic violations, generate challans, and maintain centralized violation records without manual paperwork.

Key Features:

  • Officer Login & Secure Authentication
  • Record Traffic Violations in Real-Time
  • Attach Photo/Video Evidence
  • Generate Digital E-Challans
  • Centralized Challan Database
  • Admin Dashboard & Reporting
  • Update Violation Catalog (Admin)
